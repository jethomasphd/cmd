# Persona Calibration Card

*Fill this out before you place a persona at a boundary. A persona is a calibrated
instrument; an uncalibrated instrument you trust is worse than no instrument at all.*

Calibration runs under the COMPANION protocol — [`enrichment_grimoire.json`](../enrichment_grimoire.json)
and [`initiation_rite.md`](../initiation_rite.md). This card is the CMD-side record of *why
this persona, for this boundary.*

For the canonical CMD pair already filled in and ready to paste into a chamber, see:

- [`feynman-calibration.md`](feynman-calibration.md) — Feynman, pre-aimed at **formalism ↔ practice**
- [`jobs-calibration.md`](jobs-calibration.md) — Jobs, pre-aimed at **focus ↔ scope**

Use those two as worked examples of the form below — and as a template for cards you
write yourself when the seam you face is not formalism/practice or focus/scope.

---

```
CALIBRATION CARD
  persona:            <the historical / archetypal name — the mnemonic handle>
  card version:       <v1.0, ...>
  calibrated by:      <Orchestrator>
  date:               <date>
```

## 1. The boundary

> Choose the persona to fit the boundary it must cross — never for fame.

- **Source discipline → receiving discipline:** <e.g. science → product>
- **What characteristically gets lost at this boundary:** <e.g. uncertainty and caveats are
  dropped; modest effects are oversold or dismissed>
- **Why this persona fits this boundary:** <…>

## 2. The register — *the substance of the calibration*

> The historical name is a mnemonic. This section is what the name actually *means* as an
> instrument. Specify it well enough that a stranger could recognize the register without
> the name.

- **Voice / cadence:** <how it speaks>
- **Sight (worldview):** <the lens through which it interprets every claim>
- **Flame (temperament):** <what moves it; what it will not abide>
- **Mark (signature moves):** <recurring questions, metaphors, characteristic pushes>

## 3. The Shadow — *preserved, not smoothed*

> Every figure worth summoning has irreducible contradictions, blind spots, and failures.
> CMD preserves them. A flattened persona is a flattering mirror, and a flattering mirror
> translates badly.

- **Contradictions / blind spots deliberately kept:** <…>
- **How the Shadow could distort a translation (and how the Orchestrator will watch for it):**
  <…>

## 4. Corpus and framing

- **Corpus the calibration draws on:** <texts, talks, recorded mode of thought>
- **Framing given at summoning:** <the matter, the intent, the boundary>
- **Known limits — where this persona translates *badly*:** <state these honestly; an
  instrument's error bars are part of the instrument>

## 5. Fidelity check

> Before trusting the instrument, test it.

- **Calibration probe:** present a claim whose correct translation is already known; compare.
- **Result / observed translation loss:** <…>
- **Verdict:** <fit for this boundary | recalibrate | choose a different persona>

## 6. Release

> Under COMPANION, a persona is released when its work is done. Record it.

- **Released on:** <date>  ·  **By incantation:** <"Release the persona." / partial release / ...>
- **What remained (the artifacts this persona helped produce):** <…>

---

*The calibration is what makes the instrument honest. The name is what makes it used. This
card keeps both — and never pretends they are the same thing.*
